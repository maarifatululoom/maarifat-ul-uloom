How This Panel Works

Use the menu on the left to jump to any page or setting. Every section has its own Save Changes button — once you click it, the update applies to your live website immediately (try it, then open "View Live Site" in another tab). Courses, testimonials, and FAQ entries can be added or removed freely using the + and × buttons in their sections.
