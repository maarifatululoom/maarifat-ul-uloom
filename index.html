Frequently asked questions

Questions families ask before starting

Short answers to the questions we hear most. The full list is on the About page.

See all questions

What age groups do you teach?

We welcome students of all ages, from young children just starting Noorani Qaida, to teenagers and adults learning to read, understand, or memorize the Qur'an for the first time.

Do you have both male and female teachers?

Yes, so every student and family can choose an instructor they feel most comfortable with.

How do online Quran classes actually work?

Classes take place live, one-to-one, through video call at your scheduled time, with your teacher correcting pronunciation in real time.

Is there really a free trial class?

Yes, genuinely free with no obligation; you complete one full live lesson and decide afterward.

How much does a monthly plan cost?

A simple, transparent monthly fee with no hidden charges or long-term contracts; exact pricing depends on the course and class frequency.

Are classes available in different time zones?

Yes, we teach across many countries and schedule classes at realistic times for your location.
