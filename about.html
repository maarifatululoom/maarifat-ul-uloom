Our story

An academy built around trust, structure and sincerity

Maarifat-ul-Uloom Academy was founded on a simple belief: that every Muslim, regardless of age or location, deserves access to authentic, well-taught Quranic education, delivered with warmth, structure, and sincerity.

Mission

To provide authentic, structured Quranic and Islamic education to students of all ages, delivered online with the same care and accountability as an in-person madrasah.

Vision

To become the world's most trusted online academy for Quranic learning, and, in time, a bridge between timeless Islamic knowledge and modern skills.

Students & parents

What to expect from your classes

Live, one-to-one lessons

Classes take place live, one-to-one, through video call at your scheduled time, with your teacher correcting pronunciation in real time.

What you need

Just a stable internet connection and a device with a camera and microphone.

Schedules that fit

We teach across many countries and schedule classes at realistic times for your location. To change a time, reach out in advance and we'll find a slot that fits.

Your teacher, your comfort

Male and female teachers are available. If you don't like the assigned teacher, let us know and we'll happily arrange a switch.

Progress you can see

Parents receive regular progress reports. In Hifz, progress is tracked through a structured revision cycle with regular assessments.

Flexible monthly plans

There is no long-term lock-in: pause or cancel anytime.

Simple, transparent pricing

We offer a simple, transparent monthly fee with no hidden charges or long-term contracts. Exact pricing depends on the course and class frequency: your teacher will walk you through it after your free trial class.

Frequently asked questions

Questions? We've got answers

Everything you need to know about classes, teachers, pricing, and getting started.

What age groups do you teach?

We welcome students of all ages, from young children just starting Noorani Qaida, to teenagers and adults learning to read, understand, or memorize the Qur'an for the first time.

Are your teachers qualified and verified?

Yes, every teacher undergoes a structured vetting process evaluating both Quranic qualifications and teaching ability.

Do you have both male and female teachers?

Yes, so every student and family can choose an instructor they feel most comfortable with.

How do online Quran classes actually work?

Classes take place live, one-to-one, through video call at your scheduled time, with your teacher correcting pronunciation in real time.

Is there really a free trial class?

Yes, genuinely free with no obligation; you complete one full live lesson and decide afterward.

How much does a monthly plan cost?

A simple, transparent monthly fee with no hidden charges or long-term contracts; exact pricing depends on the course and class frequency.

Can I cancel or pause my enrollment anytime?

Yes, our monthly plans are flexible with no long-term lock-in.

What if my class schedule needs to change?

Reach out to reschedule in advance and we'll find a slot that fits.

What if I don't like the assigned teacher?

Let us know and we'll happily arrange a switch.

How is Hifz progress tracked?

Through a structured revision cycle with regular assessments of new memorization and retention.

Do you teach Tajweed as a separate course?

Yes, and its principles are also woven into our Quran Reading and Hifz courses.

What equipment do I need for online classes?

Just a stable internet connection and a device with a camera and microphone.

Do you offer classes for complete beginners?

Absolutely, our Noorani Qaida course is designed for absolute beginners.

Are classes available in different time zones?

Yes, we teach across many countries and schedule classes at realistic times for your location.

How do I get started?

Reach out via WhatsApp, email, or our contact form, and we'll schedule your free trial class.
